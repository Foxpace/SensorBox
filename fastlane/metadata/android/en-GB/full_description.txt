Use your phone sensors to record motion samples from the accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, and other sensors, plus location samples from GPS. Samples are stored in CSV files on your phone. No data is sent away from your phone.

While recording, you can add annotations and customize them in settings. They are stored with the measurement metadata.

Choose a recording duration and which recording sources to use, including motion sensors with or without GPS.

More choices for the recording setup are available in settings.

Can use sensors: accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, rotation vector calculation, thermometer, barometer, luxmeter, proximity sensor, humidity meter, pedometer.

If you own a Wear OS device, you can use its sensors to record additional samples. Watch measurements can be synced to the recording archive on your phone. You can also view graphs directly on Wear OS.

Recently, the activity recognition client , Google's API, has been added, so all motion changes, which can result in change of motion state, can be recorded. Two files are generated. One with timestamps of significant changes, which resulted in change of motion state. This data are event based. Second file has timestamps with confidence of each possible activity, which can client recognize. This  data are recorded periodically with customizable frequency. 

Supported activities by activity recognition client: vehicle, bike, running, walking, still, tilting. 

The Wear OS app supports local storage, so you can record without a paired phone. Sync can be started manually from the phone app.
